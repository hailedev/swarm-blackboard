# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative — "Use X for Y"

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints apply? Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly. Include the specific choice, not just the category.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to do because of this. Mark positives with `+` and negatives with `−`.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative with one line on why it lost. Rejected options are the most useful part of an ADR six months later.
