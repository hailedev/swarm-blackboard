title: A six-week teen reading programme on a $500 budget
domain: community-planning
constraints:
  - "A public library branch in a mid-sized town; one librarian can give four hours a week, plus volunteers if recruited."
  - "Budget is $500 in total for the six weeks; the library already has a meeting room and a book collection."
  - "The target is 13-to-17-year-olds, most of whom do not currently use the library."
  - "The branch manager wants evidence of the outcome, not only attendance numbers, to decide whether to repeat it."
successCriteria:
  - "A programme design (format, frequency, content) that a non-reading teenager would plausibly turn up to."
  - "A recruitment plan that reaches teens who do not use the library today."
  - "A budget that allocates the $500 and shows what it buys."
  - "Outcome measures beyond attendance, measurable within six weeks, and how they are collected."
rubric:
  - id: design
    weight: 3
    criterion: Describes a concrete programme (what happens each week) suited to teens who do not read for pleasure.
    scale: "0 = 'a book club'; 1 = a format with no reasoning about the audience; 2 = a format argued from what draws non-readers (choice, social element, media tie-ins, creation); 3 = plus a week-by-week outline."
  - id: recruitment
    weight: 2
    criterion: Explains how teens who do not visit the library will hear about it and why they would come.
    scale: "0 = 'put up posters'; 1 = one channel; 2 = channels where teens are (schools, youth groups, social media) with a specific hook; 3 = plus a partner or peer-recruiter mechanism."
  - id: budget
    weight: 2
    criterion: Allocates the $500 to specific items that fit the design.
    scale: "0 = no budget; 1 = a total with no breakdown; 2 = a breakdown that fits the design; 3 = a breakdown with a cheaper fallback and what is in kind."
  - id: outcomes
    weight: 3
    criterion: Proposes outcome measures beyond attendance (reading behaviour, confidence, library card sign-ups, return visits) and how they are collected in six weeks.
    scale: "0 = attendance only; 1 = outcomes named but not measurable; 2 = measurable outcomes with a collection method (short pre/post survey, borrowing data, retention week to week); 3 = plus what result would justify repeating it."
  - id: feasibility
    weight: 2
    criterion: Fits four librarian hours a week and realistic volunteer effort.
    scale: "0 = needs much more staff time; 1 = plausible but unscoped; 2 = time accounted for; 3 = time accounted for with prep done once and reused."
---
A public library branch in a mid-sized town wants to run a six-week programme for 13-to-17-year-olds, most of whom do not use the library today. One librarian can give four hours a week; volunteers can be recruited; the budget is $500 for everything beyond the room and the existing collection. The branch manager will repeat the programme only if there is evidence it changed something for the participants, not just that some turned up.

Design the programme: what happens each week and why teenagers who do not read for pleasure would come, how the library reaches teens who are not already visitors, how the $500 is spent, and how the outcome is measured within the six weeks in a way that goes beyond attendance. Make sure it fits four hours of librarian time a week.
